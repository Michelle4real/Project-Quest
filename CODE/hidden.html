ARCHIVE FILE // 001 RECOVERED

Meeting Odysseus

RECOVERING FILE...

It was supposed to start with indomie.

I was minding my own business, reconnecting with an old classmate, when the conversation somehow turned to university. I told him where I was going, and apparently that was enough information for him to announce my arrival to half our old classmates.

Suddenly, people I apparently had added on Snapchat were popping up in my messages. And then one particularly bold person asked if I wanted to meet up at an Indomie joint.

There was just one problem.

It was night.

And at the time, I was very much operating under the rule that I simply did not go out at night. So, as politely as possible, I declined and explained that I didn't really come out at night.

Instead of taking it personally, he simply suggested another option: meeting at the chapel before orientation, since orientation was being held there.

And that was how we actually met.

It was a little awkward at first. I met some of our other classmates, and he was exactly what I would later come to know him as: cool, calm, suspiciously calm, collected, and genuinely funny.

Eventually, I excused myself to meet up with my friends for orientation. Before I left, he asked if we could meet up another time.

I said sure.

And somehow, that little "sure" became the beginning of a much longer story.

END OF RECORD 001
ARCHIVE FILE // 002 RECOVERED

Getting to Know You

RECOVERING FILE...

It was strange how familiar you felt.

We had technically just met, but talking to you never really felt like talking to someone I had just met. Maybe it was because we had crossed paths before, back in secondary school, even if only briefly. Maybe we simply clicked. Whatever it was, talking to you came easily.

There wasn't much of that awkwardness that usually comes with getting to know someone. We talked easily. We related to each other a lot. And somehow, I never felt like I had to constantly worry about being judged for the things I said.

You were patient with me in ways I didn't expect. You somehow had this weird ability to take and handle all my emotions, let me feel whatever I needed to feel, and then calm me down anyway.

You made me feel more free with someone I had just met than I had ever really felt before.

You even got me comfortable with calls, which is honestly impressive considering I have always been very much a text person.

You were just different, not wildly, just distinguishable.

Before long, I got used to meeting up with you in the evenings just to talk. We'd talk about adjusting to a completely new environment, everything changing incredibly fast, and all the things we never expected before coming to Veritas.

We were both trying to figure things out in our own ways, and somehow, those conversations made everything feel a little easier.

Looking back, I think that was when I really started getting used to having you around.

END OF RECORD 002
ARCHIVE FILE // 003 RECOVERED

When Things Started to Change

RECOVERING FILE...

It was a very ordinary day.

Nothing particularly special was happening. I was in my room in the hostel, doing whatever the hell I was doing, completely unaware that anything was about to change.

Then your text came up.

And somehow, I found myself smiling.

Why?

I genuinely had no idea.

You know very well that I don't process emotions the way you do. It takes me time. A lot of time, sometimes. So the fact that I was just sitting there smiling at my phone because of a text from you should probably have been my first warning.

"It was at that moment, I knew I was finished."

Looking back, that line was probably more accurate than either of us realized.

We met up later that week, or sometime around then, and I guess I agreed to whatever was happening between us. Although, to this day, I still belive that you used Edo people jazz.

Whatever you did, it eventually wore off.

But by then, it didn't really matter.

I liked where I was.

I was with you.

Things happened. Time passed. And somewhere along the way, what had started so casually became something more serious.

Of course, it wasn't all perfect. We had our little fights here and there. There were days when I know I overwhelmed you with the things I said. Days when I probably tested your patience a little too much, especially with the parties I absolutely refused to attend.

And you piss me off too. A lot, actually.

But somehow, even with all of that, you never made me feel like I was too much.

You made room for all the complicated parts of me, even on the days when I probably made that very difficult.

And I guess that's one of the things I appreciate most.

Because despite the arguments, the misunderstandings, the moments where we annoy each other and everything else that comes with us, I wouldn't have it any other way.

For no particular reason at all.

END OF RECORD 003
ARCHIVE FILE // 004 RECOVERED

The Chaos

RECOVERING FILE...

We've had our fair share of petty fights and little arguments.

Like laughing by the roadside over absolutely nothing. You watching me randomly dance at the side of the road and then pretending you didn't know me. Us mistakenly hugging people we thought we knew because they looked like someone else.

There was also the countless times we'd sit around ranting about our arch nemeses and the supposed causes of our deteriorating mental health: Mr. Isioma and Mr. Vincent. 😂

Looking back, it's funny how many random moments we've managed to collect along the way. Nothing particularly grand or planned. Just little moments that somehow became memories.

We've sincerely gone through a lot in Veritas.

END OF RECORD 004
ARCHIVE FILE // 005 RECOVERED

The Story So Far

RECOVERING FILE...

By this time last year if anyone had told me that I'd be dating someone. I would've said the person was crazy.

But here I am...

It's been a hell of an experience with you.

Not perfect. Not always easy.

But real.

Our dynamic has changed over time...

Not in a bad way.

In a simple maturing and understanding way.

And somehow, through all the chaos, the arguments, the laughter, the late conversations and all the things we never could have planned for, I'm glad it was you I got to experience it with.

And if this is only the story so far...

I think we've got a pretty good one.
