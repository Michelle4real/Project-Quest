A MESSAGE FOR ODYSSEUS

FINAL TRANSMISSION

Dear Habibi,

So...
You actually made it to the end. 😂

I'm not really sure what you're supposed to say at the end of something like this. I've spent so much time making you go through random memories, questionable evidence, things you say too often, songs, pictures, and whatever else I decided belonged in this little archive that now that we're here, I'm just supposed to... end it?

Seems a little unfair.

But I guess this is where I actually talk to you. Ughhhhhh "feelings"

When I think about us, I genuinely think it's funny how something that started so randomly became something that actually means a lot to me.
Like, out of all the people I could've met, all the people you could've met, and all the different ways our lives could've gone, somehow we ended up here.

And I don't think either of us could've predicted that.

We've changed a lot since we first met. You've definitely changed. Some things for the better. Some things...

We'll leave those ones in the archives. 😒😂

But through everything, I'm just really glad I got to know you.
Not just the version of you that everyone sees, but the random, (VERY) ragebaitful, annoying, stubborn, funny, curious, sometimes confusing person underneath all of that.
The person who can make me laugh when I wasn't even trying to laugh.
The person I can have the most random conversations with.
The person who somehow manages to annoy me and make me want to talk to him again five minutes later.
Very impressive skill, honestly.
And I know I don't always say it, but I appreciate you. I appreciate the conversations, the jokes, the stupid moments, the serious ones, the random things that probably didn't seem important at the time but somehow became memories anyway.
I appreciate the fact that I got to be part of this chapter of your life.
And now you're 18.
Which is actually crazy because I swear you were just a 16-year-old nerd fresh out of boarding school trying to act nonchalant. 😭
Now somehow you're here.
I don't know what this next part of your life is going to look like.
I don't know what you'll become, where you'll end up, what you'll accomplish, or what random problems you'll somehow get yourself into.
But I hope you give yourself time to figure it out.
I hope you don't rush yourself just because you feel like you should already have everything together.
I hope you keep the things that make you, you.
Your curiosity. Your humour. Your ability to care even when you pretend you don't. Your weird little ways of thinking about things.
And I hope that somewhere along the way, you realize just how much you've grown.
Because sometimes you're so busy trying to become the next version of yourself that you don't stop to appreciate the person you've already become.
So yeah.
That's my little piece of advice that you didn't ask for. 😂
But more than anything, I just hope you live a good life.
A genuinely good one.
Not perfect. Not some movie version of good.
Just one where you have people who love you, things that make you excited to wake up, enough stupid memories to laugh about when you're older, and enough courage to keep trying when things don't go the way you planned.
And if you ever look back at this ridiculous website years from now, I hope you laugh.
Because I hope by then you realize just how much effort went into making something this unnecessarily complicated for your birthday. 😭
Anyway. I think I've said enough (a lot).

And before you leave this little world I've made for you, here's what I really want you to know.

If you've made it this far, then I guess there's only one thing left to say.

I made all of this because I wanted to give you something that felt like us.

A little chaotic. A little ridiculous. Slightly dramatic for absolutely no reason. But yours.

And somewhere between the memories, the jokes, the songs and everything else, I hope you found a little piece of what I was trying to say.

Before this gets any more emotional and I start embarrassing myself, let me just say:

I'm really glad I met you.

And I'm really glad that somehow, out of all the people we could've become, we became us.

Now go and enjoy being 18, you old man. 😒😂

Happy birthday.✨

Transmission complete.

And now the actual ending.

Always,
Michelle.💕
